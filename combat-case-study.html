Case Study: The Three-Slot Dagger

A three-slot throwing dagger demonstrates how Infusion turns separate Techniques into a tactical chain through timing, geometry, and physical events.

Consider an expert whose dagger holds three Symbols from the same Source:

Slot 01ExplosionPlaced to create a deliberate physical event and move the carrier.
Slot 02FireHeld in reserve for a follow-on origin point.
Slot 03LightningHeld in reserve for rapid subsequent activation.

One possible chain

  1. Throw the dagger. The carrier creates a moving point of origin and asks the opponent to react.
  2. Let the opponent dodge. The response creates space, motion, and perhaps a false sense that the projectile has been solved.
  3. Activate Explosion. The effect is triggered at its Slot, producing a physical event that moves the dagger and its remaining components.
  4. Read the new geometry. The carrier is no longer where it began; its orientation and position have changed.
  5. Activate Fire and Lightning in rapid succession. Their effects are still separate, but their origin points now benefit from the explosion’s consequences.

What this is not

It is not an “explosive fire-lightning dagger spell.” It is three independent stored Techniques deployed through one carrier, joined by a fighter’s timing and the ordinary physics of the situation.

Extreme execution

At the highest level, an expert might be disarmed as a dagger flies upward, throw a separate Teleportation Template, activate it, arrive at the airborne dagger, install prepared Symbol films into its empty Slots, regain control, redirect the object, and throw it back. None of those actions needs a new systemic exception. The spectacle comes from managing movement, trajectory, activation, installation, orientation, authority, timing, and opponent position at once.

Mastery in practice

Experts do not gain more rules. They operate more rules simultaneously.

Activation

Simultaneous Effects & Order

A practitioner may activate one, two, or all three loaded Techniques from a Template. They can fire together—but they remain separate effects.

Multiple activations do not automatically fuse into a new combined spell. A Technique remains itself. The apparent “combination” comes from the practitioner’s timing, the Slot positions, the carrier’s movement, and the physical events created when one effect changes the conditions for the next.

Simultaneous activation

When two or three Techniques activate together, each originates from its own Symbol. This can create compound pressure without inventing a fourth ability. A defensively placed effect and an offensively placed effect can occur at once, or several copies of the same Technique can be spent to create repetition rather than a new category of magic.

Installation order persists

Symbols are installed in an order, and the Template retains that order. If a fighter loads 1. Thunder, 2. Lightning, then 3. Fire, the stored sequence is part of the Template’s tactical identity. When working the loadout through sequentially, that retained order supports deliberate chains and rehearsed combinations.

Physical events are the connector

A practitioner can let one Technique create a real-world event, then exploit the resulting orientation, displacement, debris, sound, momentum, visibility, or panic before activating another. The magic has not merged. The fighter has made the world between activations do useful work.

The combat grammar

Separate effects. Shared timing. One physical situation.

This principle prevents combinations from becoming rule-breaking super-spells while leaving room for extraordinarily sophisticated execution.

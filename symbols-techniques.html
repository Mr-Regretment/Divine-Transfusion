Techniques, Symbols & Slots

A Technique is learned first. A Symbol stores one prepared use of that Technique. A Spell Slot gives the Symbol a physical place to operate from.

Technique and Symbol are not interchangeable

Learning a Technique gives a practitioner the knowledge and authority to work with it. It does not mean every object they touch immediately performs it. To field the Technique, they obtain or create a corresponding Symbol and install that Symbol into a compatible Slot.

One Symbol equals one stored use. On activation, the stored use is expended: the Symbol disappears and the Slot is left empty. The practitioner still knows the Technique, but must install another prepared Symbol before that Slot can deploy it again.

The physical Slot

A typical Spell Slot is a circular disk about four centimetres in diameter. Its edge is slightly sloped, like a very small plate integrated into the carrier. The Symbol is drawn onto this surface; when installed, it becomes part of the Slot rather than a decal that can be casually lifted away.

Not a peel-off label

A Symbol cannot simply be peeled from its Slot. An attempt to tear it away instead damages or removes the entire Spell Slot. Symbol and occupied Slot function as a unified physical component.

Three separate concepts

LearnTechniqueThe ability a practitioner studies in a divine tradition.
PrepareSymbolA single physical stored use of the learned ability.
InstallSlotThe durable physical receiver integrated into a Template.

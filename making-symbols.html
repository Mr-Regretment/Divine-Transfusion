Symbol Creation & Preparation

Symbols are physical magical resources. They may come from established traditions—or be prepared by a practitioner trained in Symbol Creation.

Temples and other institutions preserve divine Techniques and the established methods for obtaining their Symbols. These traditions form a practical infrastructure: they teach, verify, manufacture, protect, and distribute the materials needed to field a learned Technique.

Symbol Creation

Advanced practitioners can study Symbol Creation and prepare their own Symbols. This does not replace learning the Technique; it extends a practitioner’s ability to stock, customise, and sustain the Techniques they already understand. Prepared Symbols can be carried as spare ammunition.

Preparation versus deployment

Creating a reliable Symbol is work performed beforehand. Installing an already prepared Symbol can be extremely fast. Do not confuse the speed of field loading with the work required to make the ammunition.

Prepared Symbol film

One especially useful format places a prepared Symbol on thin, film-like material. A skilled user can apply it to an empty Slot with a compact sequence:

Rapid installation

Stick → Press → Rip

The film stays with the Slot; the backing is removed. With enough practice, installation can happen almost instantly, making a loaded Symbol film function like magical ammunition. A fighter may carry films in a sleeve, case, magazine, or other piece of ordinary kit tailored to their style.

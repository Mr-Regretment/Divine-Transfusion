Body Templates & Self-effects

Living beings can carry Spell Slots directly in skin and flesh. This makes the body a Template—and raises the cost of poor planning.

Body Templates require creating Slots in the practitioner’s own body. They are possible, but inherently dangerous. The user must understand anatomy, the Technique’s origin point, the intended direction of force or effect, and what happens if the body is moving, restrained, or injured.

The origin-point problem

Because a Technique originates from its Symbol, an effect that is safe from a dagger may be catastrophic from an arm, torso, or hand. A bad choice can injure the user: a destructive Technique may detonate in the wrong place, teleportation may attempt to resolve into occupied flesh, or an effect may damage the very limb that carries the Slot.

Body Templates are not casual upgrades

They demand exceptional planning and anatomical awareness. A powerful Technique is only useful if its point of origin does not turn the practitioner into its first target.

Self-affecting Techniques

No general rule prevents a Technique from affecting its own Template. That is true for a body as much as for a weapon. A stored effect may move, damage, explode, or otherwise interact with the carrier on which its Slot sits. This can enable deliberate self-modification or self-destruction, but it also means the practitioner cannot assume a Template is protected by the mere fact that it bears the Symbol.

Practical disciplines

  • Anatomical mapping: understanding what lies behind, around, and beyond the intended origin point.
  • Motion rehearsal: knowing how a limb’s orientation changes while running, striking, falling, or grappling.
  • Failure planning: deciding what happens if a technique fires at the wrong instant or the limb cannot move.
  • Recovery design: accepting that even an extraordinarily durable Slot may outlast the flesh or carrier around it.

At their best, body Templates erase the delay between body and equipment. At their worst, they are a precision hazard built directly into the user.

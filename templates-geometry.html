Templates

Geometry & Concealment

A Symbol’s physical location determines the point from which its Technique originates. Template geometry is therefore part of a practitioner’s strategy.

Symbols can be positioned wherever the practitioner chooses on a Template. The same Technique behaves differently when it begins at the tip of a thrown knife rather than its rear, at the palm rather than the fingertips of a glove, or around the circumference rather than the centre of a disk. A Template is a physical map of intended actions.

Placement is a decision

Smart designs use the object’s normal movement, exposed surfaces, and likely orientation. A weapon can be built so that one Technique physically launches, turns, exposes, or repositions the carrier holding another. The magic stays separate; the design makes their interaction useful.

Hidden Symbols

Slots and Symbols may be concealed. A Template can look ordinary while carrying one or more hidden capabilities. An opponent who sees a simple throwing knife may assume it is only a projectile, while its user knows a concealed Slot contains a Teleportation Technique.

Information warfare

Experienced practitioners inspect for strange seams, unusual construction, modified surfaces, suspicious gaps, hidden plates, and implausible weight or balance. The ability to recognise a concealed Slot is a genuine combat skill.

What an observer tries to learn

  • How many Slots does this Template have?
  • Which god or Source claimed it?
  • What Techniques might be loaded—and where do they originate?
  • Are any Symbols hidden, empty, or blood-bound?
  • What physical plan does the object’s construction suggest?

Concealment is more than hiding a weapon. It is controlling the opponent’s model of what the object can become next.

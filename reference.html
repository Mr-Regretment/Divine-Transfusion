Rules at a Glance

Use this page as a compact rules check. The details and tactical consequences are explained throughout the guide.

Core identity

QuestionAnswer
Where does magic reside?In the Symbol, which stores one use of a learned Technique.
What is a Template?An ordinary physical object prepared with Spell Slots.
How many Slots?Maximum three per Template. Absolute limit.
Can a Template mix gods?No. The first Symbol establishes one Source; later Symbols must match it.
Can a person study many gods?Yes. The limitation is the immense complexity of learning and integration.
What happens on use?The Symbol disappears; the Slot remains and may be reloaded.

Physical rules

RuleResult
Slot formTypically a circular, slightly sloped disk about 4 cm across.
Slot durabilityOccupied Slots are effectively indestructible within modern technological limits.
Carrier destructionThe carrier can be destroyed while its Slots survive with stored Techniques intact.
Symbol removalA Symbol cannot be peeled off; removing it means damaging or taking the whole Slot.
Slot recoverySurviving Slots can be carried independently or installed into a new Template.
Origin pointA Technique begins at the physical location of its Symbol.

Control, timing & reloading

RuleResult
ActivationA user with authority may activate at a chosen appropriate moment; touching the carrier is unnecessary.
RangePractitioner must see the Template and be within approximately half a kilometre.
Multiple activationOne, two, or three Techniques may activate at once; their effects remain separate.
OrderInstallation order persists and supports intentional sequential chains.
Normal authorityAfter a change of active user, the prior user retains authority for about ten seconds; then the new user gains it.
Blood-bound authorityRemains with the creator and can persist after death, recognising a same-lineage reincarnation.
ReloadingEmpty Slots accept prepared legal Symbols; Symbol film supports rapid stick → press → rip loading.

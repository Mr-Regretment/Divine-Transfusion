Overview

Infusion is a martial magic system in which divine Techniques are learned, stored as physical Symbols, and deployed through ordinary objects called Templates.

The central philosophy

The gods provide the Techniques. The practitioner provides the skill. The Template provides the platform.

No one is simply born with “lightning powers.” A practitioner chooses a divine tradition, learns its Techniques, then builds a fighting style from physical ability, ordinary equipment, positioning, timing, preparation, and experience. A dagger is only a dagger until it receives a Symbol. A glove is only a glove until its Slots are loaded.

The magic resides in the Symbol. The Template is an ordinary carrier prepared to receive it.

The three rules that shape everything

Rule 01Three slotsEvery Template holds a maximum of three Spell Slots—no exceptions for size, skill, or legend.
Rule 02One godThe first Symbol locks a Template to one divine Source. A person may study more than one.
Rule 03One use eachWhen a stored Technique is activated, its Symbol vanishes. The empty Slot can be reloaded.

These limits do not make Infusion simple. They make it expressive. A fighter can hide a teleportation Symbol in a knife, trigger it after the knife is caught, lose the carrier but recover the nearly indestructible Slots, or rebuild a loadout in seconds with prepared Symbol film. The system rewards the person who understands their tools better than anyone else.

What this guide covers

Begin with the foundational relationship between Sources, Techniques, Symbols, Slots, and Templates. Then follow the practical questions: who controls a thrown object, what survives an explosion, how a Symbol is reloaded, where a Technique originates, and why an apparently ordinary object may be the most dangerous thing in the room.

Reading principle

Whenever a rule seems powerful, ask three questions: where is the Symbol? who has authority? and when does it activate? Those answers usually reveal the tactical stakes.

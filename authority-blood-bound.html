Blood-bound Symbols

A practitioner trained in Symbol Creation can bind a Symbol permanently to themself by incorporating their own blood into the special Symbol ink.

A blood-bound Symbol remains associated with its creator. Unlike an ordinary Symbol, it does not simply switch activation authority when another person takes the Template. This makes blood-binding ideal for personal equipment, signature carriers, and tools a practitioner cannot afford to surrender during a transition.

Blood-binding changes authority

It does not make a Symbol infinite, remove the one-use consumption rule, exceed the three-slot cap, or permit a second Source on the same Template.

Binding after death

Blood-binding persists after the creator’s death. A blood-bound Symbol can remain associated with its original owner indefinitely. If that person is reincarnated within the same lineage, the Symbol can recognise the reincarnation and return authority to its original creator in a new life.

Inheritance without transfer

A blood-bound Symbol can wait through generations—not for an heir, but for the return of its origin.

Strategic implications

Ancient blood-bound Slots can become sleeping assets, vault keys, sources of suspicion, or impossible relics. A thief may capture the physical component yet never gain authority to activate it. A later descendant or reincarnation may be the only person able to awaken it. This turns heritage into an active mechanical question rather than merely a story of ownership.

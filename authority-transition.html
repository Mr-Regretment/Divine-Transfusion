Authority

User Authority & Transition

Generic Templates do not have permanent owners. Their normal Symbols recognise the person actively fighting with the Template.

Authority governs activation, not possession in a legal or sentimental sense. A practitioner with authority over a Symbol can trigger its stored Technique. The system makes room for disarms, catches, thefts, and recoveries by allowing control to move between active users rather than binding every ordinary Symbol forever.

The transition window

Authority does not flip the instant an object changes hands. If Person A throws a loaded three-Symbol dagger and Person B catches it, Person A retains control during a short transition period of approximately ten seconds. After that interval, the Symbols recognise Person B as the current user. Person B can then activate their stored Techniques.

Why the delay matters

Ten seconds is long enough for a specialist to use a lost, airborne, or captured Template as an ongoing tactical piece. A fighter can throw a dagger, let an opponent catch it, and still choose whether to activate the stored Symbol before the other person gains control. Conversely, a captor who survives the transition inherits a potentially useful—or dangerous—set of loaded Techniques.

Authority is a combat resource

Track the timer whenever a Template changes active hands. A disarm does not immediately remove the previous user’s options, and a catch does not immediately grant the new user safe control.

What authority does not decide

Authority does not prevent a person from installing a normal, non-blood-bound Symbol. A person may load a blank Template held by someone else. It also does not override the Source lock or the three-slot limit. Those are properties of the Template itself.

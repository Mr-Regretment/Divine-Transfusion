The One-God Rule

A person may study many gods. A Template may carry Techniques from only one Source.

A blank Template has no divine association and is compatible with any god. The first Symbol installed changes that: it establishes the Source to which that carrier belongs. From that moment onward, every other Symbol installed into the Template must belong to the same god.

Jurisdiction, not effect

This is a rule about origin, not surface resemblance. If two gods possess an identical or nearly identical Technique, they still belong to separate divine jurisdictions. A Technique from God B cannot be loaded beside the similar Technique from God A merely because both produce the same practical result.

The distinction to remember

The One-God Rule applies to Templates, not people. A multi-tradition practitioner can carry one Template from God A, another from God B, and a third from a forbidden Source—provided each individual carrier stays faithful to a single Source.

What happens when a Symbol is spent?

Consumption empties a Slot but does not make the Template blank again. The carrier remains associated with the Source that claimed it. Reloading must use a Symbol from that same Source. The rule belongs to the Template’s identity, not merely to the Symbols still visible on it.

Design consequence

A broad practitioner does not combine gods by mixing them into one object. They combine traditions by switching objects, arranging multiple carriers in the field, and timing their separate divine loadouts to interact physically.

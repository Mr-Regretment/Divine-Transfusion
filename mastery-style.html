Fighting Style & Mastery

Infusion is a martial system as much as a magical one. The same god and the same Techniques can produce radically different fighters.

A fighting style combines Technique, body, Templates, Symbols, sequence, timing, and strategy. A practitioner may integrate divine Techniques into striking, grappling, footwork, movement, weapons, positioning, feints, and equipment work. What appears to be “magic style” is usually the sum of many trained decisions.

Three stages of mastery

01KnowledgeThe practitioner knows the Technique and can perform it.
02IntegrationIt is incorporated into movement, Templates, sequencing, and combat.
03ExpressionThe practitioner has developed a personal interpretation.

Signature Techniques

A Signature Technique is a highly personalised application of a known Technique. It does not require inventing a wholly new supernatural ability. A practitioner takes a standard divine Technique and applies it with such distinctive geometry, timing, equipment, or sequencing that the use becomes associated with them. The Technique remains shared; the expression becomes personal.

Same technique, different magic

At the highest level, two practitioners may appear to wield entirely different magic while using the same divine Techniques. Their Templates, movement, preparation, and decisions make the familiar ability unrecognisable.

What skill determines

  • Activation speed and precision.
  • Timing of effects and exploitation of physical events.
  • Template geometry and practical Slot placement.
  • Reloading speed and choice of replacement Symbols.
  • Concealment, inspection, and deception.
  • Authority management and Template switching.
  • Adaptation when the prepared plan breaks.

The actual ceiling

A practitioner becomes extraordinary not because the system gives more options, but because they use the options they already have better than anyone else.

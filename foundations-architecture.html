The Infusion Chain

Infusion separates the supernatural ability from the object that deploys it. Understanding that separation prevents most category errors in the system.

God / Source
The supernatural jurisdiction to which a Technique belongs.
Technique / Spell
The actual learned supernatural ability. In this guide, Technique and Spell mean the same thing.
Symbol
The physical representation of one stored use of a Technique.
Spell Slot
The physical disk on a Template onto which a Symbol is installed.
Template
The ordinary physical object that carries one to three Spell Slots.
Practitioner
The trained person who prepares, controls, and activates the loaded Template.

Where the magic is

The Template itself is not inherently magical. Steel, porcelain, wood, plastic, fabric, armor, jewelry, a tool, a projectile, or a section of the body can all serve as Templates if they are prepared with Slots. The magical component is the Symbol held in the Slot.

Object and effect are separate
Stored Symbol
Spell Slot
Empty Slot

A Symbol is consumed on use. The Slot—the durable point of connection—remains available to be refilled.

Why the chain matters in combat

Because effect and carrier are distinct, breaking a carrier does not necessarily remove a Technique. Because the physical location of the Slot establishes the Technique’s origin point, the shape of the object becomes part of the casting decision. Because Symbols can be prepared separately, a fighter can carry them as ammunition and rebuild an emptied Template under pressure.

Think in layers

When evaluating any piece of equipment, separate what the object is, which Source it is locked to, which Symbols are presently loaded, and who can activate them. They are related but not the same question.
